# Olist Store Customer Policy (fictional, for this project)

## Returns
- Customers may return an item within 30 days of the delivery date.
- Only orders with status "delivered" can be returned.
- Items must be unused and in original packaging.
- Digital goods and perishable items cannot be returned.

## Refunds
- Refunds go back to the original payment method.
- Refunds are processed within 7 business days after the returned item is received.
- Refunds up to R$100 can be approved automatically.
- Refunds above R$100 require approval from a human support agent.
- Shipping (freight) is refunded only if the item arrived damaged or the wrong item was sent.

## Late deliveries
- If an order arrives after its estimated delivery date, the customer may request a refund of the shipping fee.
- If an order is more than 15 days late, the customer may request a full refund without returning the item.

## Cancellations
- Orders can be canceled free of charge before they are shipped.
- Orders that are already shipped cannot be canceled; the customer must wait for delivery and use the return process.

## Damaged or wrong items
- Report damaged or wrong items within 7 days of delivery.
- The customer is refunded in full, including shipping, without returning the item.

## Contacting support
- Support is available Monday to Saturday, 9:00 to 18:00 (Brasília time).

## Order status guide
- created, approved, invoiced, processing: the order has not shipped yet. It can be canceled for free.
- shipped: the order is on its way. It cannot be canceled. Share the estimated delivery date.
- delivered: eligible for return and refund under the rules above.
- canceled: no further action. If the customer was charged, the refund is issued within 7 business days of cancellation.
- unavailable: the item could not be supplied. The customer is refunded in full automatically.

## Lost orders
- If a package never arrived, is missing, or got lost in transit, check how late it is. An order still not delivered 20 days after its estimated delivery date is treated as lost.
- For a lost order the customer gets a full refund including shipping, with no return required.
- Lost-order refunds above R$100 still need human approval.

## Payment methods
- Credit card: refunded to the same card.
- Boleto: refunded by bank transfer; the customer must provide bank details to a human agent.
- Voucher: refunded as store credit.
- Debit card: refunded to the same account.
- Orders paid in installments are refunded proportionally across the installments already charged.

## Partial refunds
- If an order has several items, the customer can return a single item.
- The refund for a returned item is the price of that item, plus its share of shipping only if the item was damaged or wrong.

## Refund limits and abuse prevention
- A customer may have at most 3 refunds approved in any 90-day period. Further requests go to a human agent.
- Refund requests for orders older than 90 days after delivery are always declined, except for lost orders.
- The assistant must never promise a refund before the eligibility check is done.

## When to escalate to a human
- Refunds above R$100
- Boleto refunds (bank details needed)
- Customers who report fraud or a charge they do not recognize
- Complaints about seller behavior or product safety
- Any case the assistant cannot resolve from this policy

## Out of scope
- The assistant cannot change delivery addresses after an order has shipped.
- The assistant cannot give legal advice or discuss other customers' orders.
